# Slide 3: When a Measure Becomes a Target

A perfect cadmium yellow circle in the upper-left quadrant. Overlapping it, a grid of thin black horizontal and vertical lines forms a rigid measurement matrix that extends across the entire composition. Where the grid overlaps the circle, the yellow fragments into broken rectangular shards. Small vermillion red squares scatter at the collision points. Ultramarine blue ground on the right third. Bauhaus hard-edge constructivist, flat depthless space, Mondrian-meets-Kandinsky tension, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, texture, noise

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
