# Slide 5: What This Paper Argues

Three concentric rings — innermost vermillion red, middle ultramarine blue, outermost cadmium yellow — each ring drawn with different stroke weights (thin, medium, thick). Between each ring, the parchment white ground breathes. A bold black diagonal bar enters from the upper-right corner and stops precisely at the outer ring's edge, suggesting entry. Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction, flat color, hard edges, centered balanced composition with asymmetric accent, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D rendering, glow

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
