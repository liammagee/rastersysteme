# Slide 6: The Three Loops

Three concentric rings on constructivist black ground — innermost ring thin vermillion red, middle ring medium ultramarine blue, outer ring thick cadmium yellow. Each ring is broken by a single gap at a different rotational position, creating three offset openings. Small warm white circles mark each gap like nodes. The composition is perfectly centered, monumental, and still. Bauhaus geometric abstraction inspired by Kandinsky's Several Circles, hard edges, flat unmodulated color, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, shadows, atmospheric effects, soft edges, texture

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
