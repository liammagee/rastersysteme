# Slide 7: Intellectual Debts

Multiple geometric fragments arranged like an archaeological display — a vermillion red semicircle, an ultramarine blue right angle, a cadmium yellow arc, a black cross, a white square — all precisely spaced on a warm parchment ground. Thin black connecting lines run between them like a constellation map. Each form is a different vocabulary for the same underlying structure. Bauhaus collage composition, Moholy-Nagy constructivist, flat hard-edge, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, atmospheric effects

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
