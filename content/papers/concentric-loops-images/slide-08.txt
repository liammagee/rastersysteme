# Slide 8: 2. The Inner Loop

A single tight vermillion red circle, bold and thick, centered on constructivist black ground. Inside the circle, a precise grid of thin cadmium yellow horizontal lines fills the interior like a scanning mechanism. A small ultramarine blue dot sits at the exact center. The composition is minimal, focused, mechanical — a closed system. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, no shadow, maximum precision, Kandinsky-inspired, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, shadows, glow, atmospheric effects, multiple objects outside circle
