# Slide 10: The Refine-Step Pattern

A vermillion red circle on the left, connected by a black horizontal arrow-bar to an ultramarine blue square in the center, connected by another black arrow-bar to a cadmium yellow triangle on the right. Below, a thin black arc curves from the triangle back to the circle, completing the cycle. All on warm parchment white ground. The three forms are precisely aligned on a horizontal axis. Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction, flat mechanical precision, Moholy-Nagy inspired, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, recognizable objects, shadows, atmospheric effects, soft edges
