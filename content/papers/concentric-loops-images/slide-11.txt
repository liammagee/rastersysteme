# Slide 11: Convergence Behavior

Three vermillion red arcs of decreasing curvature stacked vertically — the top arc steep and dramatic, the middle arc moderate, the bottom arc nearly flat. Each arc starts from the left edge and terminates against a vertical ultramarine blue line on the right. The spaces between arcs are filled with cadmium yellow. Black constructivist ground. The composition reads as energy diminishing, a system approaching rest. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, asymptotic rhythm, 16:9.
