# Slide 12: 3. The Outer Loop

Two concentric rings — inner vermillion red, outer ultramarine blue — on warm parchment white ground. The outer ring is broken into four arcs with gaps between them, as if being pried open from the outside. Small cadmium yellow triangles wedge into each gap, pointing inward. A black diagonal bar crosses the upper-right quadrant. The inner ring remains intact but the outer system is under interrogation. Bauhaus constructivist, flat hard-edge, Kandinsky-inspired, 16:9.
