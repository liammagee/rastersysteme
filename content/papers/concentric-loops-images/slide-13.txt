# Slide 13: The Score-Perception Gap

On the right half, the same circle's outline repeated but shattered into jagged geometric fragments — vermillion red shards, ultramarine blue splinters, black wedges — scattered but still recognizable as the ghost of a circle. A thin black vertical line divides the two halves. Warm parchment ground. Bauhaus constructivist hard-edge abstraction, flat color, stark duality, 16:9.
