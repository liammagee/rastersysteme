# Slide 15: Schon's Conversation with the Situation

Two geometric forms in dialogue across a warm parchment ground — a vermillion red semicircle on the left faces a cadmium yellow semicircle on the right, their flat edges toward each other with a narrow gap between them. In the gap, a thin ultramarine blue zigzag line oscillates vertically like a signal passing between them. Black horizontal bars frame top and bottom. Bauhaus constructivist hard-edge, flat exchange rhythm, call-and-response composition, 16:9.
