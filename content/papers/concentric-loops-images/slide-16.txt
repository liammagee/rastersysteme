# Slide 16: Case Study: The Invented Labels

A grid of cadmium yellow rectangles arranged in rows on constructivist black ground — uniform, orderly, systematic. Three of the rectangles are vermillion red instead, scattered among the yellow at irregular positions, intruders in the system. Thin ultramarine blue crosshairs mark each red rectangle. The composition is clinical, diagnostic — anomalies identified within a field of conformity. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, systematic grid, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, labels, letters, words, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D

Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
