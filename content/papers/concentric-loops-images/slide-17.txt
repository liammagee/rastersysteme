# Slide 17: Case Study: Invisible Images

A large ultramarine blue square dominates the center of a constructivist black ground. Inside the blue square, a slightly different shade of blue forms a circle — nearly invisible, almost lost in the ground. A cadmium yellow border traces the hidden circle's edge on only one quarter of its circumference, revealing what the eye almost misses. Thin vermillion red corner brackets in the composition's four corners. Bauhaus hard-edge, flat color, study in near-invisible contrast, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, photographs, images, shadows, high contrast, glow

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
