# Slide 18: The Wireframe as Intent Artifact

A precise grid of thin black lines on warm parchment white ground, forming a 6x4 matrix of rectangles. Some cells are filled with flat vermillion red, others with ultramarine blue, others with cadmium yellow — a compositional plan made visible. One cell's fill extends beyond its grid boundary, overlapping the adjacent cell. Diagonal black lines cross the overflowing zone. Bauhaus constructivist flat hard-edge, Mondrian-adjacent grid composition, structural intent made geometric, 16:9.
