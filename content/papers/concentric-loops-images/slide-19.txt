# Slide 19: Double-Loop Learning

Two concentric loops rendered as geometric paths — inner loop a tight vermillion red rectangle with rounded corners, outer loop a larger ultramarine blue rectangle with rounded corners. The inner loop has a small cadmium yellow arrow-triangle indicating clockwise rotation. The outer loop has a larger cadmium yellow arrow-triangle indicating counter-clockwise rotation. Constructivist black ground. The two loops rotate in opposition. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, dynamic tension, 16:9.
