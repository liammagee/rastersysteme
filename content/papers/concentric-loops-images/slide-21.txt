# Slide 21: The Score That Went Back Up

A cadmium yellow angular line moves from left to right across constructivist black ground — rising steeply, then dropping sharply at a midpoint, then rising again to a higher position. The drop zone is marked by a vermillion red vertical bar. The rise zones are flanked by thin ultramarine blue horizontal reference lines. The composition reads as a trajectory interrupted and recovered. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, angular kinetic energy, 16:9.
