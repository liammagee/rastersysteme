# Slide 22: 4. The Outer-Outer Loop

Three concentric rings — innermost vermillion red thin, middle ultramarine blue medium, outermost cadmium yellow thick — all on constructivist black ground. The outermost ring pulses with radiating black lines extending outward like a starburst, suggesting expansion beyond the frame. The inner two rings are stable and precise. The composition vibrates between containment and transcendence. Bauhaus Kandinsky-inspired geometric abstraction, hard edges, flat primary color, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, shadows, glow, lens effects, atmospheric haze

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
