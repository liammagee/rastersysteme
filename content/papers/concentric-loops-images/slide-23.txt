# Slide 23: When the Outer Loop Isn't Enough

Two concentric rings — vermillion red inner, ultramarine blue outer — on warm parchment ground. Outside both rings, three cadmium yellow geometric forms (triangle, square, diamond) float in the margins, unconnected to the ring system, invisible to it. Black dashed lines attempt to reach from the rings toward the yellow forms but stop short. The blind spots exist just outside the system's reach. Bauhaus hard-edge flat abstraction, constructivist tension between seen and unseen, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, atmospheric effects, eyes
