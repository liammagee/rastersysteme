# Slide 24: From Sampling to Exhaustive

A horizontal row of 36 thin vertical bars on constructivist black ground. Five bars in the center are cadmium yellow — the sample. The remaining 31 bars are ultramarine blue — previously invisible. One bar far to the right is vermillion red — the missed anomaly. A thin cadmium yellow horizontal line now extends across all 36 bars, connecting them into a single exhaustive sweep. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, systematic coverage, 16:9.
