# Slide 26: The Design Database as Institutional Memory

A grid of geometric forms — circles, squares, triangles — in vermillion red, ultramarine blue, and cadmium yellow, arranged in a structured matrix on constructivist black ground. Thin white lines connect forms across rows and columns, creating a web of relationships. At the bottom-right, a new form (a small cadmium yellow hexagon) enters the grid, extending the system. Bauhaus hard-edge flat abstraction, archival constructivist composition, accumulated structure, 16:9.
