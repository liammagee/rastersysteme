# Slide 27: Deutero-Learning

Three concentric rings on warm parchment ground — vermillion red innermost, ultramarine blue middle, cadmium yellow outermost. But the outermost ring is not a ring — it is composed of five smaller geometric forms (small circles, triangles, bars) arranged in a ring shape, each form a different element that was learned. The ring itself is the lesson: the system learned how it learns. Black accent lines radiate between the component forms. Bauhaus Kandinsky-inspired hard-edge abstraction, meta-structural, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, shadows, brains, minds, 3D, atmospheric effects

Aspect: 16:9
