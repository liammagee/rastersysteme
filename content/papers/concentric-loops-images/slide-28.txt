# Slide 28: 5. Tools and Infrastructure

Bold geometric forms arranged as structural components on constructivist black ground — a vermillion red horizontal beam, an ultramarine blue vertical column, cadmium yellow diagonal braces, small white connection nodes at each junction. The forms compose an abstract architectural framework, a scaffold. The construction is mid-process, neither raw materials nor finished structure. Bauhaus Moholy-Nagy constructivist hard-edge, flat industrial geometry, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, tools, wrenches, gears, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D perspective
