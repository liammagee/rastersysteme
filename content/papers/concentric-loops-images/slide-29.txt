# Slide 29: rastersysteme

A 60-column grid of thin cadmium yellow vertical lines on constructivist black ground, evenly spaced, extending the full width. Horizontal vermillion red bars of varying widths span different column groups, creating a rhythmic pattern of occupied and empty zones. An ultramarine blue rectangle highlights one zone group. The grid is the system — precise, modular, Swiss-rational. Bauhaus meets Swiss typography grid, hard-edge flat geometric abstraction, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, curved lines, type specimens
