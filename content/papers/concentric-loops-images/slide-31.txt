# Slide 31: The Evaluation Instruments

Three ultramarine blue geometric forms in a row on constructivist black ground — a circle, a square, and a triangle — each containing different internal structures: the circle has concentric rings (simple), the square has a grid matrix (structured), the triangle has intersecting diagonal lines (complex). Thin cadmium yellow baseline connects all three. Vermillion red dots mark equivalent positions in each form. Three instruments, one measurement. Bauhaus hard-edge flat abstraction, 16:9.
