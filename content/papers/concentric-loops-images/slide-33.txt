# Slide 33: 6. Results

A large cadmium yellow circle on constructivist black ground, positioned in the upper-right quadrant. From its lower-left edge, three geometric beams radiate outward — vermillion red, ultramarine blue, and white — like searchlights illuminating the dark lower-left of the composition. The beams are straight-edged trapezoids, not gradients. The yellow circle is the source, the beams are findings. Bauhaus hard-edge flat geometric abstraction, dramatic constructivist composition, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, shadows, light beams, spotlights, 3D, atmospheric glow

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
