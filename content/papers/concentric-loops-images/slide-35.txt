# Slide 35: Three Meanings of 10/10

Three identical cadmium yellow circles arranged horizontally on constructivist black ground — same size, same color, same position relative to their local space. But each sits in a different frame: the first in a thin vermillion red square (loose), the second in a medium ultramarine blue square (tighter), the third in a thick white square (strictest). The circles are the same; the contexts transform their meaning. Bauhaus hard-edge flat abstraction, identical forms in different frames, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, numbers, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, atmospheric effects
