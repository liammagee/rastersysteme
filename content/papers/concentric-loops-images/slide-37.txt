# Slide 37: The Cybernetics Recursion

Three concentric rings on constructivist black ground — vermillion red innermost, ultramarine blue middle, cadmium yellow outermost. But the entire three-ring system is itself contained within a larger thin white ring, and that white ring is contained within an even larger thin black ring visible only by a subtle shift in black tone. The recursion implies infinite depth. A single diagonal white bar crosses all rings. Bauhaus Kandinsky-inspired hard-edge geometric abstraction, recursive nesting, 16:9.
