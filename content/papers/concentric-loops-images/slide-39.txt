# Slide 39: A General Framework

Three concentric rings — vermillion red innermost (tight, fast), ultramarine blue middle (broader), cadmium yellow outermost (widest) — on constructivist black ground. Each ring has a different line treatment: inner is solid, middle is dashed, outer is dotted — three timescales rendered as three line rhythms. The rings are perfectly concentric, the canonical diagram of the framework. White accent points at compass positions. Bauhaus geometric abstraction, definitive statement, hard-edge flat, 16:9.
