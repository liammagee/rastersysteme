# Slide 40: Recursive, Symbiotic, Generative

A constructivist composition on black ground where the three-ring motif (vermillion, blue, yellow) appears at three different scales simultaneously — a large version in the center, a medium version in the upper-right, and a small version in the lower-left. Thin white connecting arcs link corresponding rings across scales. The pattern is self-similar: fractal without being fractal. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, recursive self-similarity across scale, 16:9.
