# Slide 42: Second-Order Cybernetics

Three concentric rings (vermillion, ultramarine, cadmium yellow) on warm parchment ground. But each ring is not merely a line — each ring is composed of smaller geometric forms (tiny squares, circles, triangles) that together trace the ring's path. The observer is built into the observed system. A black geometric wedge interrupts the outer ring, the point of intervention. Bauhaus Kandinsky-inspired hard-edge abstraction, observer-embedded-in-system, flat constructivist, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, eyes, mirrors, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, atmospheric effects

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
