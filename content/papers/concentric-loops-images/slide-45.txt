# Slide 45: What We Found

Three geometric forms on warm parchment ground, arranged in a descending diagonal from upper-left to lower-right. A vermillion red triangle (the specific finding), an ultramarine blue square (the structural finding), a cadmium yellow circle (the general principle). Each form is solid, flat, definitive. Thin black connecting lines trace the diagonal. Generous white space surrounds each form. Bauhaus constructivist hard-edge, flat declarative composition, three truths, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, atmospheric effects, crowded composition

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
