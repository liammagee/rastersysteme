# Slide 46: The Remaining Frontier

A constructivist black ground. The three concentric rings (vermillion, ultramarine, cadmium yellow) are positioned in the left half, complete and stable. The right half is empty — pure black, unmarked, open. A single thin white dashed line extends from the outer yellow ring toward the right edge, reaching into the unknown space but not arriving. The frontier is the absence. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, asymmetric openness, unfinished reach, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, atmospheric effects, horizons, landscapes

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
