# Slide 47: Postscript

The three concentric rings (vermillion, ultramarine, cadmium yellow) on warm parchment ground, slightly off-center to the left. The outer cadmium yellow ring has a single small gap — a deliberate imperfection, an honest incompleteness. A tiny vermillion red dot sits in the gap, marking the score that is not 100%. The composition is quiet, intimate, self-aware. The system that evaluated itself found itself wanting. Bauhaus hard-edge geometric abstraction, flat color, quiet imperfection, Kandinsky late-period restraint, 16:9.

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, numbers, recognizable objects, shadows, 3D, atmospheric effects, perfection

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus constructivist geometric abstraction
