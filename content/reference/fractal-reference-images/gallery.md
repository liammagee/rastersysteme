# Image Prompts: fractal-reference

**Style:** bauhaus
**Visual Thread:**
- Palette: #D40000 vermillion red, #FFD700 cadmium yellow, #003DA5 ultramarine blue, #1A1A1A near-black, #F5F5F0 warm white
- Motif: Intersecting circles and orthogonal bars arranged on an implied grid — Kandinsky's tension between curved and straight elements, recurring as a structural rhythm across all compositions
- Atmosphere: Flat, high-contrast, no gradients. Hard-edged forms cast no shadows. Compositions feel architectural and deliberate, as if arranged on a drafting table under even studio light. White or black ground.
