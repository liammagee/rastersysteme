# Slide 5: Dense Bullet Slide

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, dense field of twelve horizontal bars in alternating vermillion red, ultramarine blue, and cadmium yellow, tightly packed with minimal spacing on near-black ground, creating a sense of compression and visual weight. Bars vary slightly in length creating a ragged right edge. The overall mass feels heavy, saturated, almost claustrophobic. Constructivist density study, Kandinsky-inspired, hard edges, flat primary colors, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, recognizable objects, whitespace, breathing room, 3D rendering

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
