# Slide 12: Image Slide

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, a large cadmium yellow rectangle with rounded-square proportions centered on near-black ground, containing a single ultramarine blue circle and a vermillion red triangle overlapping at the center — the Bauhaus primary shape triad. Thin white border around the yellow rectangle. The image-within-an-image as constructivist frame. Hard edges, flat color, Kandinsky composition, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, photographs, cameras, recognizable objects, 3D rendering

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
