# Slide 13: Links

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, four ultramarine blue rectangles arranged vertically on warm white ground, each connected by a thin vermillion red diagonal line to a cadmium yellow circle positioned at the right side of the composition. The diagonal lines create a dynamic fan pattern. Connectivity and reference as constructivist network geometry. Moholy-Nagy inspired, hard edges, flat color, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, chains, links, recognizable objects, 3D rendering, arrows

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
