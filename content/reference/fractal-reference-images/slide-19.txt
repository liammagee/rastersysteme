# Slide 19: Data-Dense Slide

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, eight vertical bars of increasing height arranged left to right on near-black ground — alternating vermillion red and ultramarine blue — forming an ascending staircase pattern. Thin cadmium yellow horizontal lines cross at regular intervals creating a measurement grid. Dense numerical energy expressed as pure constructivist geometry. Moholy-Nagy precision, hard edges, flat color, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, numbers, letters, charts, recognizable objects, 3D rendering, curves

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
