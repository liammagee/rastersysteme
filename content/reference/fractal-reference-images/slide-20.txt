# Slide 20: Absence-of-Bad vs Presence-of-Good

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, two fields side by side on warm white ground — left field is a gray rectangle (absence, neutral, empty), right field is a vibrant vermillion red rectangle with an ultramarine blue circle and cadmium yellow triangle inside (presence, vitality, intention). A thin black vertical line divides them. The contrast between void and substance. Constructivist duality, hard edges, flat color, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, faces, thumbs, recognizable objects, 3D rendering

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
