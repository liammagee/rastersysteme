# Slide 30: The Gap

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, two near-identical vermillion red rectangles on near-black ground separated by a deliberate gap of empty black space — the void between them is the subject. A single thin cadmium yellow horizontal line hovers in the gap. Ultramarine blue barely visible at the far edges. The distance is the signal. Constructivist negative space study, Kandinsky-inspired tension, hard edges, flat color, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, bridges, connections, recognizable objects, 3D rendering, touching shapes

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
