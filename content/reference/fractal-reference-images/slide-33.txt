# Slide 33: Maximum Density Slide

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, the frame packed to absolute capacity — vermillion red bars, ultramarine blue circles, cadmium yellow triangles, black rectangles, and white lines layered and overlapping on every square inch of the composition. No breathing room. Every position occupied. The visual equivalent of maximum information density. Constructivist saturation point, hard edges, flat color, no whitespace, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, recognizable objects, 3D rendering, whitespace, minimalism, calm

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
