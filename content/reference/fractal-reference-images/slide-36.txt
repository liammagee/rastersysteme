# Slide 36: What Improved, and Who Improved It

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, two distinct zones on warm white ground — lower zone of precise ultramarine blue and cadmium yellow geometric shapes arranged in orderly rows (machine precision), upper zone of a single large vermillion red brushstroke-width bar placed at a slightly imperfect angle (human judgment). Thin black line divides the zones. Agency and authorship as constructivist composition, hard edges, flat color, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, hands, people, faces, recognizable objects, 3D rendering, actual brushstrokes

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
