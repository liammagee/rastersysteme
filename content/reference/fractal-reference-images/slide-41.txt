# Slide 41: Arnheim Balance Test

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, intentionally asymmetric composition on warm white ground — a large dense mass of overlapping vermillion red and ultramarine blue rectangles occupying the left two-thirds, counterbalanced by a single small but intense cadmium yellow circle in the upper right. The asymmetric weight creates dynamic tension. Near-black thin line suggests a fulcrum point off-center. Arnheim visual balance as constructivist study, hard edges, flat color, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, scales, seesaws, recognizable objects, 3D rendering, symmetry, centered composition

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
