# Slide 43: Recursive

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, three concentric rings on warm white ground — outermost ring is thin cadmium yellow (system level, slow), middle ring is medium ultramarine blue (deck level, medium), innermost ring is thick vermillion red (slide level, fast). Each ring contains smaller versions of the same three-ring pattern at four compass points. Near-black center point. Recursion across timescales as constructivist mandala, hard edges, flat color, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, clocks, recognizable objects, 3D rendering, spirals, natural patterns

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
