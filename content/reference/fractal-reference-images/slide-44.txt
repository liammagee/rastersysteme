# Slide 44: Symbiotic

Bauhaus geometric abstraction, two interlocking shapes on near-black ground — a vermillion red gear-like form with orthogonal teeth meshes with an ultramarine blue gear-like form, their teeth perfectly interleaving. Cadmium yellow fills the spaces between the teeth where they meet. Neither shape is complete without the other. Symbiosis as constructivist mechanics, Moholy-Nagy inspired, hard edges, flat color, 16:9 aspect ratio

Negative: organic forms, photorealism, soft gradients, decoration, text, letters, actual gears, machines, recognizable objects, 3D rendering, rounded teeth

Aspect: 16:9
Style: Bauhaus
