Welcome to Introduction to Generative AI for Education! In 2026, despite abundant AI materials, there remains real value in active discussion of the topic. This course offers multiple perspectives—historical, technological, practitioner, critical, pedagogical, futurological—without imposing a single doctrine. Each week explores one lens; you'll notice tensions between them. This week is introductory: no readings, just orientation and shared experience. Sessions run 2 hours 50 minutes (theory ~30 min, discussion ~30 min, practice ~30 min) with a 10-minute break.
