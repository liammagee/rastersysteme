What kind of intervention? Any of the following artefacts **[1]**:
  - Presentation (15-20 slides)
  - A0 Conference Poster 
  - Research Experiment / Paper
  - School Curriculum
  - Policy Document
  - Comic strip
  - Movie
  - Mock product pitch / ad campaign for a new AI service
