Welcome to Introduction to Generative AI for Education!

What's this course about? Why does this course exist? In 2026, in some sense, we no longer need a course introducing us to Generative AI - for education or for anything else. It might even seem, for those of us following the latest developments of OpenAI's ChatGPT, Anthropic's Claude, or new agentic systems like OpenClaw, as though we are even reaching the point of singularity in the field of education.

This course naturally contests this characterization. The approach we will take will lean in on the idea that "generative AI" is an object we need to approach from several perspectives or lens. Each week after this one will outline one of these perspectives. These lens do not comprise a single coherent doctrine of AI - and I will be encouraging you to notice and document the tensions between them.

Six Lens. Seven Weeks. One Intervention.

The course was first run by me last year, and the approach will be largely the same. However I have expanded what was 90-120 minute sessions into a full 2 hours 50 minute session, as I felt we were frequently short of time for discussions and practice. So nominally we'll blocking out the time if we need it - with a 10 minute break roughly half way through.

Each session, including today, will be run as a workshop - a mix of theory, discussion and practice.
COURSE OBJECTIVES

What You Will Do

1Develop understanding of generative AI through six thematic lens: Historical, Technological, Practitioner, Critical, Pedagogical, Futurological
2Respond to AI with a self-styled "intervention" addressing a pedagogical or social challenge
3Address a range of scenarios raised by AI in education
COURSE DESCRIPTION

Explores applications of Generative AI in Education. Topics include: AI predecessors; Large Language Models and statistical approaches to meaning; machine learning; chatbot architectures and prompt engineering; fine-tuning; multimodal AI; guardrails including bias, hallucinations, explainability, IP, privacy and security; and applications of Generative AI in education
