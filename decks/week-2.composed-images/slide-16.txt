# Slide 16: Crisis in Higher Education

Editorial photography, 35mm film grain. A classical Ionic column fragment, abstracted in Saul Bass style, parchment-toned, visibly cracked and tilted slightly off plumb. Prussian blue fracture lines run diagonally upward from base to capital. Around the base: scattered copper fragments of eroded institutional trust. Raking side light emphasizes textured damage. Melancholic but measured — erosion, not catastrophe.
