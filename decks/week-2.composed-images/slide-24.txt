# Slide 24: WEEK 2 — Continuous to Discrete, Analog to Digital

Editorial photography, 35mm film grain. Split composition: left half shows a smooth flowing copper curve — organic, continuous, analog; right half shows the same curve rendered as a pixelated staircase of silver-grey blocks — digital, stepped, lossy. At their boundary: a subtle transitional zone where one bleeds into the other. Raking light emphasizes the contrast. The philosophical cost of digitization.

Negative: literal pixels on screen, computer display, binary code text, HDR, clip art, stock digital imagery, text labels
