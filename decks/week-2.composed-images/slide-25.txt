# Slide 25: WEEK 2 — Financial Markets, 2025

Editorial photography, 35mm film grain. An abstract market chart rendered as ascending and descending copper lines on a parchment field with a thin prussian blue coordinate grid. Vertical axis unmarked; horizontal axis compressed into shallow time. The overall waveform resembles a vital-sign monitor. Raking light from the right. Suggestive of markets as biological phenomenon, not literal financial data.
