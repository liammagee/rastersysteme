# Slide 27: WEEK 2 — Historical Palimpsest

Editorial photography, 35mm film grain. Aged parchment with layers of partially erased diagrams beneath the surface — faint arcs, coordinate grids, and circles from different historical eras, each generation of marks in a slightly different tone. Prussian blue the most recent stratum, copper the oldest, deepest. Sharp raking light at oblique angle reveals the depth of the palimpsest. History written over history.

Negative: literal archaeological photograph, text overlays, dramatic lighting effects, HDR, clip art, stock history imagery
