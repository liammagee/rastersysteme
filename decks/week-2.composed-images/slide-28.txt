# Slide 28: WEEK 2 — Bridge Between Eras

Editorial photography, 35mm film grain. Two isolated geometric forms at opposite ends of a parchment field: a circle at left (organic, warm copper) and a square at right (rational, prussian blue). A single fine silver thread connects them across the empty middle distance. The intervening space is the subject. Raking light, long shadows from both forms. Minimalist, Saul Bass. Distance as meaning.

Negative: literal bridge photography, text labels, crowded composition, HDR, clip art, stock connectivity imagery, decorative elements

Aspect: 16:9
