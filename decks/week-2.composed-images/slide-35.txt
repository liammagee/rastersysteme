# Slide 35: Case Study: Geist in the Machine

Editorial photography, 35mm film grain, shallow DOF. Two simplified Saul Bass profile faces in mirror opposition — one warm copper, one prussian blue — their forms slightly overlapping at center. Between them: a luminous silver zone, the intersubjective space of recognition. Raking light illuminates the copper face fully; the blue face remains in partial shadow. The phenomenology of mutual recognition rendered as geometry.
